# Forge texture variants

Eight ImageGen albedo sources for the Forge material catalogue. Godot imports
them as GPU-compressed 1024 px textures with mipmaps; the source PNGs remain at
their generated 1254 px resolution for non-destructive reimport.

Generated on 2026-08-28 with the `stylized-concept` taxonomy and the
`tileable game texture` asset type.

## Shared prompt constraints

Each prompt requested one square seamless albedo for a stylized but believable
ancient Mediterranean game environment, an orthographic front-on or top-down
surface scan, completely even neutral lighting, no perspective, directional
shadow, highlight, baked ambient occlusion, border, focal element, text, symbol,
logo, or watermark. Left/right and top/bottom edges were requested to connect
continuously, with a natural distribution and no obvious repeated cluster or
square-grid artifact.

## Subject prompts

- `limestone_ashlar_clean.png`: pale sun-baked limestone ashlar blocks, varied
  block sizes, subtly chipped edges, fine beige mortar, faint mineral stains and
  age variation.
- `limestone_coastal_weathered.png`: weathered coastal limestone masonry,
  irregular medium stones, salt bleaching, subtle ochre mineral streaks, a few
  hairline cracks and recessed pale mortar.
- `marble_cool_worn.png`: cool white and pale-grey marble slabs with restrained
  blue-grey veins, a worn matte temple floor and subtle age discoloration.
- `sandstone_honey_ashlar.png`: warm honey and ochre sandstone ashlar blocks,
  moderately worn desert-city or fortress masonry, fine sand-coloured mortar,
  subtle erosion and mineral variation.
- `volcanic_rubble_dark.png`: dark charcoal volcanic rubble masonry, irregular
  porous stones, sparse cool-grey mortar and subtle iron-brown weathering.
- `earth_trampled_dry.png`: dry compact earth heavily trampled by sandals, carts
  and soldiers, dusty umber and muted ochre soil, embedded grit, irregular scuffs
  and shallow worn tracks.
- `gravel_coastal_mixed.png`: coastal gravel with pale limestone pebbles,
  muted grey-blue stones, sparse warm sand and tiny shell fragments in varied sizes.
- `flagstone_damp_mossy.png`: damp aged dungeon flagstones, irregular cool-grey
  stones, dark worn joints, restrained olive moss and lichen along cracks, and
  subtle moisture discoloration.
